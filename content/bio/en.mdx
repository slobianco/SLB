Cielo Rojo began in a small home studio where one musician spent years writing, recording, and producing every guitar line, drum hit, bass groove, and vocal take alone.

What started as a way to process personal change became a ten-song passage through transformation, loss, and rebirth. The recurring image was always the same: a blood-red sky at the edge of night, beautiful and impossible to ignore.

The music draws on the physical force of classic heavy metal, the restless structures of progressive records, and melodic textures carried from Latin American folk traditions. Nothing was added for polish alone. Every part had to move the story forward.

## The long way around

The record grew between late nights, rebuilt arrangements, erased takes, and the slow discovery of a voice that could hold all of it together. Working alone made the process exacting, but it also made the final statement indivisible: every sound belongs to the same point of view.

Cielo Rojo is a studio project for now, but these songs were built for a room full of people. The next chapter begins when the first note leaves the speakers and becomes everyone else's.
