Making a record alone does not make the room quiet. It fills it with arguments between every version of yourself.

The guitarist wants the riff heavier. The drummer wants the silence before it to last one beat longer. The vocalist needs the key to move. The producer has to decide which instinct serves the song rather than the moment.

Cielo Rojo was built by letting those voices disagree, then returning to the story for the final answer. The process was slower than expected and more honest because of it.
