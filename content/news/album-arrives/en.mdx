After years of writing, rebuilding, and recording, **Cielo Rojo** is ready to leave the studio.

The ten-song record follows one continuous arc from first light to descent, collapse, and renewal. Every instrument and vocal was written and performed by the same person, with the production shaped around the feeling of standing beneath a sky that looks like it is on fire.

The final release date and listening links will be announced here. Until then, the title track offers the first doorway into the record.
